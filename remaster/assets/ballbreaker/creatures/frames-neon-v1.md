## Prompt

Use case: style-transfer.
Create a faithful HD remaster sprite sheet of image 1, original_frames.png. Image 1 is the authoritative design AND pose/layout reference. Image 2 is only the material/lighting style reference from the current Ball Breaker remake.
Deliver ONE wide transparent PNG sprite sheet with EXACTLY FIVE sprites in a single evenly spaced horizontal row, left to right matching image 1. No text, labels, grid, background, floor, scene, or cast shadows. Transparent pixels between all sprites, generous padding, no clipping. Equal width cells and consistent scale/registration appropriate to animation. Frames 2 and 4 must be identical matching the original repeated pose.
Faithfulness is more important than embellishment. Preserve the unusual original cartoon reptile/monster HEAD silhouette: big rounded rear cranium, short blunt right-facing snout, one large vertical oval eye near the front, small pointed ear/horn, chunky square teeth, stubby neck, exaggerated jaw. Do NOT turn it into an elongated realistic dinosaur/dragon or add a body, arms, wings, armor accessories, spikes, realistic scales. Keep original relative head/neck/jaw proportions, outline occupancy and positioning within each frame, including the head rising in frame 3. First head almost closed mouth; second moderately open with jutting lower jaw; third wide open with dropped long lower jaw; fourth exact repeat of second. The fifth distinct small figure must faithfully retain its angular arrowhead-like pointed cap/head facing right, small bright eye, narrow stalk/neck, broad horizontal collar and squat rounded/stepped base from the original; do not invent a new character or turn it into a mushroom.
Style: crisp polished 2.5D arcade sprite art, dark translucent emerald/green glass-like faces, restrained faceted volume, thin luminous mint-green edges, soft very localized green glow, pale mint filled highlights and readable original facial features. Simpler and closer to the reference pixels than the existing dinosaur in the game screenshot. Smooth bevels around the original stepped silhouette without losing its distinctive shapes. Soft directional light upper-left, dark mouth interior, a few blocky pale mint teeth, large expressive oval eye with dark vertical pupil. Preserve retro charm and readability at small size. Moderate dimensional shading, not photorealistic. Material and rendering quality consistent across all five sprites. Keep glows compact so silhouette and bite shapes stay legible. Original silhouette/pose fidelity takes priority over extra detail.
